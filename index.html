07A few helpful answers

Good to know.

What types of events do you handle?+

We plan and coordinate weddings, traditional ceremonies, birthdays and social celebrations, corporate events, and event styling. Tell us what you are planning and we can discuss the right support.

How early should I book?+

As early as you can, especially for a wedding or a date with high demand. Get in touch with your event date and we can talk through availability and next steps.

Do you travel outside Benin City?+

We serve Benin City and surrounding areas. Share your event location in your enquiry so we can confirm whether we can support it.

Do you handle event-day coordination?+

Yes. Event coordination is part of our services. We can discuss the level of support that fits your plans and the event day.

Can you work with my preferred vendors?+

We can coordinate with your preferred vendors. Let us know who is already part of your plans when you get in touch.

How do I get a quotation?+

Send us an enquiry with the event type, date, location and the support you have in mind. We will follow up to understand your needs and discuss a suitable quotation.
